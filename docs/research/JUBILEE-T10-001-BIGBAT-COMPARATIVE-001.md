## 1. Mesopotamian clean-slate edicts

Old Babylonian rulers issued misharum / andurarum-style reforms addressing social and economic disorder. Scholarship describes royal intervention involving remission of some obligations and release from debt bondage. These acts were associated with royal authority, often accession or political events, rather than a fixed sevenfold recurring clock.

Controls:
- Pamela Barmash, “Biblical and ancient Near Eastern law,” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/rec3.12262
- Robert Irwin, “Rethinking Economics and Education: Exponential Growth and Post-Growth Strategies,” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/edth.12256

### Structural comparison

~~~text
PUBLIC / SOVEREIGN ACT         YES
DEBT RELEASE                    YES

REVERSAL OF SOME ALIENATIONS    ATTESTED IN RELEASE TRADITIONS
FIXED RECURRING 7×7 CLOCK       NO
AUTOMATIC CALENDAR EXECUTION     NO

The major contrast is constitutional:

~~~

## 2. Solon's seisachtheia

Later Greek sources attribute to Solon a “shaking off of burdens” involving relief from debt, removal of debt markers (horoi), changes to debt bondage, and recovery of some Athenians sold abroad. The detailed economic mechanism and extent of land restoration remain historically disputed.

Useful control:
- scholarship on Athenian horoi and the limits of the evidence, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2041-5370.2000.tb01949.x

### Structural comparison

~~~text
PUBLIC CONSTITUTIONAL REFORM     YES
DEBT RELIEF                      YES
PERSONAL DEBT-BONDAGE REFORM     YES
RETURN / REPATRIATION            REPORTED
LAND RESTORATION                 DISPUTED / NOT SAFE AS SIMPLE CLAIM
FIXED PERIODIC CLOCK             NO
RECURSIVE SABBATH STRUCTURE      NO
~~~

### Pressure result

Debt cancellation + personal liberation + return is not unique to Jubilee.

But Solon's reform is a historical constitutional intervention, not a recursive calendrical recurrence.

Therefore:

> **RELEASE + RETURN CAN EXIST WITHOUT JUBILEE'S CLOCK.**

## 3. Modern bankruptcy

Modern U.S. bankruptcy is a useful nonreligious control because it sharply distinguishes stages.

Official U.S. Courts sources:
- https://www.uscourts.gov/court-programs/bankruptcy/bankruptcy-basics/chapter-7-bankruptcy-basics
- https://www.uscourts.gov/court-programs/bankruptcy/bankruptcy-basics/discharge-bankruptcy-bankruptcy-basics

Chapter 7 provides, in bounded form:

~~~text
FILE PETITION
  ↓
AUTOMATIC STAY
  collections generally stop

ADMINISTRATION
  estate / exemptions / creditor process

DISCHARGE
  personal liability for dischargeable debts ends

RECORD
  proceeding and order remain historically real
~~~

A discharge does not erase every legal relation: some debts are non-dischargeable and valid liens may survive.

### Pressure result

This is a very strong genericity hit against:

~~~text
release without erasure
hold before final release
bounded non-universal discharge
publicly attributable gate
~~~

Those are not Jubilee-specific ideas.

What bankruptcy lacks is Jubilee's specific combination of:

~~~text
7×7 recurrence
fixed T10 proclamation
hereditary land return
kinship return
~~~

New engine law:

## 4. Transitional justice / South African TRC

The South African Truth and Reconciliation Commission separates several functions through distinct institutional channels:

~~~text
TRUTH / HUMAN-RIGHTS VIOLATIONS
AMNESTY
REPARATION / REHABILITATION
~~~

Official sources:
- https://www.justice.gov.za/trc/
- https://www.justice.gov.za/trc/trccom.html

Amnesty could release a perpetrator from prosecution for a particular act while truth findings and victim-reparation processes remained distinct.

Scholarship emphasizes that transitional justice includes truth commissions, amnesty, reparations, prosecution, apology, and institutional reform as separate but interacting mechanisms:
- Ceva & Murphy, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.12663
- McEvoy & Mallinder, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6478.2012.00591.x
- Roche, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-4560.2006.00448.x

### Pressure result

Another direct genericity hit:

~~~text
RELEASE != TRUTH ERASURE
AMNESTY != REPARATION
ENDING LIABILITY != RESTORATION COMPLETE
~~~

This strongly supports the *usefulness* of separating Jubilee Engine operations, but weakens any claim that the separation is uniquely biblical.

New law:

> **MERCY, MEMORY, AND RESTITUTION MUST REMAIN SEPARATELY TYPED.**
