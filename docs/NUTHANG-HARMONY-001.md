## Purpose

Prove that three independently sourced fictional capacities can be composed into a candidate chord while one omitted permission remains a distinct required gate. Then prove that one local invitation, voluntary alias decisions, an attempted action, a report, a different alias's review, and a return can remain reconstructible without treating any of those statements as verified physical facts.

*Example*: A grower's ingredient supply, a kitchen steward's space, and a carrier's transport are sufficient to **propose** a neighborhood supper. All three offers must be independently accepted and the venue owner must separately approve. One actor's refusal is terminal in this specimen: no substitute is invented. After a fictional attempt, only the carrier may report and only a different alias may review. An accepted completion produces a CANDIDATE_ONLY reusable-method indicator, **not** verified delivery.

## Run locally without accounts

From repository root, with Node.js 22+:

    node --test harmony/engine.test.mjs
    python3 -m http.server 8080

Then open http://localhost:8080/harmony/ in a modern browser. The page imports harmony/engine.mjs and uses WebCrypto on the secure localhost origin. No login, registration, API key, backend, analytics, browser storage, external URL, or HTTP API call is needed. No online deployment is implied.

## Ownership and authority

1. **Source cut** — FIXTURE is a frozen fictional source with three distinct offer aliases and source refs. A separate packet cannot silently substitute it. The exhaustive bounded composer selects the smallest sourced subset covering the declared capabilities; it does not infer real-world causal effectiveness.
2. **Independent gates** — the three offer decisions and the venue-owner authorization are separate. The offered kitchen capability does not imply the owner's permission to hold a supper there.
3. **Refusal** — a recorded refusal ends this specimen. No model or operator rewrites it, forces participation, or invents a replacement.
4. **Synthetic history** — each event is an ordered SHA-256-linked append-only entry binding the frozen fixture, sequence, previous hash, and event body. Deterministic cold replay checks the hashes and allowed transitions. Browser state is ephemeral, in memory only.
5. **Claims** — invitations, consents, attempts, reports, reviews, and returns are fictional alias assertions. Distinct aliases are not independently authenticated people, and valid hashes do not establish attendance, bodily action, or real-world delivery.
6. **Inheritance** — only an accepted completed report separately reviewed in the synthetic loop produces CANDIDATE_ONLY. No act is submitted to Book of Acts compileAct or closeNuBlock or to any live ledger. It does not broadcast, fundraise, notify, or publish a recording.
7. **Export/import** — JSON export downloads locally. JSON import is limited to 128 KB and verifies the frozen fixture, event ancestry, derived disposition, and non-claims. It is not posted to a server. Do not enter sensitive information in this prototype.

## Proof gates

The included Node test suite covers deterministic composition, source-cut freeze, separate owner permission, invalid and duplicate actor claims, missing consents, terminal refusal, no early reports/reviews, reporter/reviewer alias separation, cold replay, hash tampering, derived-outcome tampering, no silent weakening of non-claims, partial/disputed return, and one-way closure.

Before a field pilot: independent review; privacy and safe-use design; owner-operated adapters; authentic identity and consent; actual recipient and host permission; independent physical confirmation; food safety considerations; a baseline using ordinary human conversation; and a fresh owner decision on whether to deploy or connect anything.

## Research hypothesis

A composer may help independent people identify a missing bridge that enables a beneficial act. To claim a **harmonic dividend**, compare observed real-world outcomes with a simple human or classical-matching baseline. A beautiful graph or a deterministic demo alone is not such evidence.

**The particular survives every projection. The people retain their right to leave.**
